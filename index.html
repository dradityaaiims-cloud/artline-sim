Tips

  • Do a flush test first. A shape problem with an abnormal flush test is the system's damping; a shifted trace with a normal flush test is levelling or zeroing; an odd shape with a normal flush test is the patient.
  • Zeroing and levelling are different acts: zeroing removes the transducer's offset (open to air); levelling fixes the hydrostatic reference (height). Neither fixes the other.
  • Drag in the right panel to rotate the view; scroll to zoom.
